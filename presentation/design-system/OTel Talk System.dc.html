01

The idea

A trace is a line with events on it. That is the whole system. Every slide hangs off one horizontal signal axis — a hairline rule that fades out at both ends — and content attaches to it as nodes (circles) and spans (stadium bars). Nothing gets boxed in.

Where a surface is genuinely needed it takes the 45° chamfer lifted from the OpenTelemetry mark's angled joints — one corner cut, never four corners rounded. Diagonals split section slides. Right angles are reserved for imagery and code.

01Amber is the signal — it marks the one thing the room should read first. Never more than one amber emphasis per slide.
02Blue is structure — axes, spans, diagram plumbing, secondary type on dark.
03Grounds alternate — ink for openers, dividers, waterfalls and code; paper for anything the room should read slowly.

Amber fails small-text contrast on paper — use it for fills, rules, numerals and type above 40px only; for amber-toned body copy drop to #8A5B00. No pure white, no pure black. The capybara's browns are mascot-only and never enter the interface palette.

Geometry

chamfer 40
Top-right corner cut at 45°. Panels, image masks, tags. 40px at slide scale, 26px in documents.
Span bar — a full stadium; height sets the radius. This is the list primitive: use it instead of bullets.
Node — filled, hollow, haloed. Marks an event on the axis; the halo means "you are here".
Diagonal split at ~72° — the ground change for section dividers and before/after comparisons.

The mascot

A capybara in an OTel cap — a nod to Adriana's own artwork, cut out on transparency so it drops onto either ground. It is punctuation, not decoration: the cover, the closing slide, and at most one mid-deck moment where the room needs a breath. Never on a data slide, never smaller than 90px, never more than one per slide.

It rides the signal axis like any other node — park it sitting on the rule, not floating above it. assets/capybara-mascot.png
